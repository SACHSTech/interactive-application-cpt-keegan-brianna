## DESCRIPTION
- Our program is a collection game. There will be rectanglular blocks falling down from the sky and your goal is to catch them. Occasionally there will be special
 power-ups that give you more points than the standard rectangle. If you fail to catch a block it will NOT respawn. The goal is to catch as many as possible!

## INTERACTION
- To move the pot, you can either hold down A/D or hold down the LEFT and RIGHT arrow keys.

## LIMITATIONS / INCOMPLETE FEATURES
- The pot may move on its own without you holding down a button due to the lag of the program
- There is no "end screen" for when no more blocks fall down
